Music4Wellbeing (M4W) is a small UK charity in Kent, in South East England.
M4W gives group therapeutic music sessions and related creative activities,
such as singing, dance and arts. Experienced practitioners lead the sessions.
The beneficiaries are people in need because of physical or mental ill health,
disability or age. This includes older adults, people with dementia or
Parkinson's, their carers, and people who are isolated or lonely.
